# CARBON ACTUAL ECOSYSTEM — Master Context
*Canonical source of truth — committed Sept 5, 2026. Companion to CANON.md.*

## 1. WHAT CARBON ACTUAL IS

Carbon Actual is a human-centered digital ecosystem for organizing **people, organizations, resources, relationships, assets, liabilities, services, opportunities, value and transactions** into one continuously connected environment.

**Carbon = Being.**
**Actual = Becoming.**

The ecosystem begins with the real person or real organization and builds outward from them.

The goal is not to replace the person with software. The goal is to give the person a coherent digital environment in which their identity, relationships, resources, obligations, activities, assets, opportunities and value can be understood and coordinated.

The foundational principle is:

> **The human remains the principal owner and administrator of their existence and value. The ecosystem organizes, protects, connects and unlocks that value.**

The ecosystem is therefore designed around **continuity, consent, ownership, relationships, value creation and circulation**.

---

## 2. THE CORE IDEA

Most modern systems divide a person's life into disconnected applications: identity, banking, education, business, government, social media, assets, marketplaces, contracts, communications, healthcare, family, estate, investment, professional activity, digital accounts.

Carbon Actual connects these fragmented pieces.

The user should be able to see:

**Who I am → what I own → what I owe → what I can do → what I provide → what I need → who I relate to → what opportunities exist → what value is moving → where value can be unlocked.**

The ecosystem continuously discovers relationships between those things.

An apparently isolated asset can become part of a marketplace opportunity. A skill can become a service. A relationship can become an opportunity. A liability can become something that is negotiated, transferred, settled, insured, financed or otherwise managed. A dormant resource can become productive. A transaction can create a relationship. A relationship can create another service. A service can create another market.

This is why Carbon Actual is an **ecosystem rather than a collection of applications**.

## 3. THE ADMINISTRATOR PRINCIPLE

The ecosystem has a strict governance rule:

## The human is the ultimate authorized administrator of their own ecosystem.

AI does not become the owner. AI does not silently acquire rights. AI does not independently publish, transfer, sell, delete or materially alter the person's assets or identity.

AI may: read, analyze, classify, organize, monitor, identify relationships, identify opportunities, recommend, calculate, simulate, notify, prepare actions.

Human authorization is required for consequential actions such as: publishing, transferring ownership, deleting, deploying, executing transactions, entering binding agreements, granting material authority, exposing sensitive information, moving money or value.

The system is therefore **consent-first and approval-aware**.

## 4. THE VALUE PRINCIPLE

Carbon Actual treats **value as more fundamental than the asset representation of that value**.

Value can exist before someone has properly identified, represented, priced, financed, tokenized or traded it.

> **Value-before-asset.**

The ecosystem seeks to discover value that already exists but is fragmented, dormant, invisible, inaccessible or poorly coordinated.

Examples: unused equipment, unused land capacity, professional skills, educational knowledge, inventory, intellectual property, local production, community networks, distribution relationships, purchasing power, future receivables, contracts, data, verified reputation, time, access, services, relationships.

Carbon Actual turns those realities into structured, discoverable and interoperable objects without confusing the digital representation with the underlying real-world ownership.

## 5. KEY ECONOMIC PRINCIPLES

### Decimalization — Value can be represented at finer levels rather than only as large indivisible units.

### Fractionalization — A valuable object or economic interest may be represented as divisible participation where legally and structurally appropriate.

### Democratization — Access to markets, information, services, opportunity and financial mechanisms should not depend exclusively on existing institutional privilege.

### Tokenization / Minting — A real-world entity, relationship, right, contribution, resource, service, agreement or digital object can be represented by a controlled digital token or identity object. Minting does not automatically mean public blockchain issuance. The system may begin with an internal database ledger and later connect representations to blockchain networks where appropriate.

### Ledger — Every meaningful economic or governance event should be traceable: ownership, authorization, contribution, transfer, settlement, obligations, agreements, transactions, token issuance, token movement, reputation/value events, provenance.

### Consent — No important economic or identity action should occur outside the authority granted to the relevant human or organization.

### Provenance — The system must be able to answer: Where did this come from? Who owns it? Who authorized it? What changed? Who participated? What value moved?

## 7. VALUE AS THE SAFE HAVEN

> **Assets and liabilities remain associated with the person or originating entity; value is the safe haven through which the ecosystem organizes them.**

The platform should not unnecessarily absorb ownership. The ecosystem can instead: verify, classify, value, connect to services, list, finance, fractionalize where appropriate, tokenize, place into escrow workflow, connect to a buyer, connect to an investor, or use as part of a broader economic relationship.

**Ownership ≠ Representation ≠ Access ≠ Utility ≠ Liquidity ≠ Value**
