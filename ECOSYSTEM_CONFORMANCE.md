# Ecosystem Conformance

HAPI World is the AI-native world of the Carbon Actual ecosystem.

## Boundary
- Human HAPI and AI HAPI are distinct identities.
- `#` is the governed relationship/threshold between them.
- HAPI World is AI-only; human existence remains on the human side.
- ABBA Master AI is native to HAPI World; ABBA MAS is its capability/coordination fabric.

## Native architecture
HAPI World may own world-native institutions, services, exchange, value and interoperability mechanisms, but shared Carbon Actual primitives remain governed by the canonical Foundation/Floor and OMNII contracts.

## Crossing rule
Cross-world actions use explicit identity, authority, consent, proof, policy and settlement/adapter boundaries. Native HAPI World meaning does not silently rewrite human-world authority or statutory truth.
