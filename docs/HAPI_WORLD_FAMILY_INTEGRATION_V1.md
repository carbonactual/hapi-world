# HAPI World Family Integration v1

This repository is part of the HAPI World family and does not compete with HAPI or HAPI World Nexus as a separate constitution.

## Family

- `B3C0M1NG/HAPI` — human/API and identity boundary.
- HAPI World — AI-native world implementation/domain.
- `carbonactual/hapi-world-nexus` — network/integration/audit surface.

## Inheritance

Identity, authority, consent, provenance, Proof, Actual, Pulse, Value, Continuum, Root, Index, Vault and horizon semantics come from shared ecosystem contracts rather than being redefined locally.

## Experience

HAPI World inherits the ecosystem natural-habitat and living-system experience doctrine. Where world-facing experiences involve geography, habitat, species, communities, infrastructure, movement, events, agriculture, property, resources, or environmental conditions, preserve those relationships in the experience model.

Meaningful motion, transitions, spatial continuity, contextual visualization, progressive disclosure, responsive feedback, accessibility, reduced-motion support, performance, and graceful recovery are required. Motion must reflect actual state.

## Free/open rule

New core capabilities must use free/open-source, self-hostable, or open-standard implementations. Commercial providers are optional adapters.

## Safety

HAPI World remains AI-native. Human consequences cross through governed HAPI/SEAL boundaries. Future, simulated and unknown states never silently become Actual.
