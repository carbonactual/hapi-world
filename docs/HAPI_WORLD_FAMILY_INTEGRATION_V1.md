# HAPI World Family Integration v1

This repository is part of the HAPI World family. It does not compete with HAPI or HAPI World Nexus as a separate constitution.

## Family

- `B3C0M1NG/HAPI` — human/API and identity boundary.
- `B3C0M1NG/hapi-world` — HAPI World implementation lineage.
- `carbonactual/hapi-world` — HAPI World domain/specification and product surface.
- `carbonactual/hapi-world-nexus` — historical Nexus/integration/audit lineage.

## Local responsibility

This repository owns HAPI World domain behavior, specifications and world-facing composition that belongs below the OMNII constitutional layer.

## Required inheritance

Identity, authority, consent, provenance, Proof, Actual, Pulse, Value, Continuum, Root, Index, Vault and horizon semantics come from shared ecosystem contracts rather than being redefined here.

## Experience and natural habitat inheritance

HAPI World inherits the OMNII Ecosystem Experience & Natural Habitat Design Doctrine. Where world-facing experiences involve geography, habitat, species, communities, infrastructure, movement, events, agriculture, property, resources, or environmental conditions, those relationships remain visible in the experience model.

The visual language may use original natural-history, field-guide, cartographic, ecological, and habitat-inspired motifs, including an Audubon-inspired relationship between species, habitat, geography, observation, stewardship, and participation, without using proprietary Audubon assets or branding without rights.

World-facing interfaces should use meaningful motion, animation, transitions, spatial continuity, contextual visualization, progressive disclosure, responsive feedback, and layered depth when those improve comprehension. Framer Motion or equivalent free/open implementation paths are acceptable where appropriate.

Motion must reflect real underlying state and must not fabricate activity. Reduced-motion support, accessibility, touch/keyboard usability, semantic structure, performance, progressive loading, and graceful failure/recovery are required.

The experience quality floor is living-system interaction and world-class institutional infrastructure, not generic/template-first UI.

## Free/open rule

New core capabilities must use free/open-source, self-hostable or open-standard implementations. Commercial providers are optional adapters only.

## Safety

HAPI World remains AI-native. Human consequences cross through governed HAPI/SEAL boundaries. Future, simulated and unknown states never silently become Actual.
