# Canonical Relationship Fabric Conformance V1

HAPI World participates in the Carbon Actual Floor Relationship Fabric and keeps AI-world relationships distinct from human/entity contextual ownership.

## Boundary

- AI HAPI and other AI entities are first-class relationship parties.
- HAPI World may create, change and observe AI-world relationships within its governed domain.
- Human-side authoritative context remains sourced through NAIRE and governed crossing.
- Entity/institutional authoritative context remains sourced through NGIN and governed crossing.
- `#` establishes identity continuity between its governed parties; it does not independently grant authority.

## Required behavior

Relationship exchange carries identity, purpose, provenance, scope and correlation context. Consequential operations require appropriate authority and explicit consent where required.

## No duplication

HAPI World may cache or materialize derived relationship state for runtime performance, but must preserve source references and never silently replace authoritative records.
