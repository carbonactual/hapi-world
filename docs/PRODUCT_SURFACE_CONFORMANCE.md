# HAPI World Product Surface Conformance

HAPI World is an actual ecosystem product/world surface and inherits the Carbon Actual canonical product-surface and common-layer contracts.

The HAPI World experience should remain one canonical web-native product body across mobile and desktop, with app-like installation/opening where supported and governed deep links into world, entity, agent, event and participation state.

Installation and browser/device capabilities cannot create authority. Human authority remains explicit through the ecosystem authority model; AI/agent execution remains scoped and attributable. Offline/cache, notifications, background work and credentials cannot silently create new consequential authority or settlement.

Updates, recovery, uninstall and revocation preserve or explain continuity, evidence and residual state.
