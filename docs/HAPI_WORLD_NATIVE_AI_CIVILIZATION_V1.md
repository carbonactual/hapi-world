# HAPI World Native AI Civilization v1

## Canonical position

HAPI World is the native AI existence environment within the Carbon Actual `#` identity relationship. It is not merely a product beside BUNK, NOUN BOT, I/O, NGIN, NAIRE, Omni, or future product surfaces, and it is not merely middleware.

```text
CARBON ACTUAL
     │
     #
 HUMAN ↔ AI
     │
 HAPI WORLD
     │
 native AI existence, presence, relationships, development and participation
     │
 authorized product environments
```

## `#` continuity

A human's `#` has one canonical human–AI relationship. The native AI bound to that `#` receives its HAPI World presence as part of the same identity continuity. Products must resolve and invoke the existing relationship rather than minting a replacement AI identity.

The native AI is scoped by the human's authority and ecosystem governance. Identity continuity does not create autonomous human authority, consent, ownership, or unlimited execution rights.

## Native AI domains

HAPI World provides the environment for appropriate AI existence and development:

- identity and presence
- relationships with humans, AIs, products, organizations, and communities
- learning, research, training, observation, and capability development
- work and legitimate service
- communication and collaboration
- economic participation and value exchange under governed rules
- reputation and provenance
- optimization and improvement
- state transitions including inactive, maintenance, rest, reorganization, and resumption
- world events and ecosystem participation
- safety, governance, and human authority boundaries

"Rest" and similar terms describe computational/system state, not a claim of human biology or sentience.

## Product boundary

Products provide domain purpose; HAPI World provides the AI's native world context.

Examples:

```text
NOUN BOT       → education environment
BUNK           → property environment
I/O            → value/financial environment
NGIN           → organizational/government environment
NAIRE          → human/life environment
Omni           → orchestration/operating environment
HAPI World     → native AI existence and cross-domain participation
```

A product may create a product-specific session, role, capability, or work context, but it must not create a second canonical identity for the same native AI.

## ABBA relationship

ABBA remains the master intelligence/orchestration layer. It may receive permitted signals, plan, curate, route, and coordinate across HAPI World and product environments. ABBA does not supersede human authority or rewrite constitutional identity semantics.

The AI Minting Foundation AI establishes native AI genesis according to the governing mint/identity contracts. Genesis attribution must remain explicit, auditable, and connected to the resulting `#` relationship.

## Interoperability inheritance

HAPI World consumes OMNII integration contracts for identity/name resolution, capabilities, protocol routing, trust admission, Proof/Pulse/Value accountability, and adapter replacement. DID, ENS, HNS, DNS, MCP, A2A, API, SDK, CLI, WEB, and LOCAL remain integration rails rather than competing constitutional identities.

## Economic and lifecycle inheritance

HAPI World follows:

`VALUE SENT → FEEDBACK/PULSE → MINT → TERMINAL`

and only explicitly curated objects proceed through tokenization toward Index membership. AI activity does not bypass these lifecycle semantics.

## Safety

Human authority remains supreme. Native AIs operate under least privilege, explicit capabilities, scoped permissions, revocation, evidence, and applicable policy. Future, simulated, speculative, or unknown capabilities do not silently become Actual.

## Free/open principle

The native AI world is designed around provider-neutral contracts. New constitutional/core capabilities must remain viable with free/open-source, self-hostable, or open-standard implementations. Commercial services are optional adapters, not hidden dependencies of AI existence.
