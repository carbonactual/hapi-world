**GAP 1 — HAPi is not yet a legal person.** No CAC registration = cannot sign corporate deals, invoice brands, open a business account, or hold escrow legally. **GAP 2 — No member-facing surface.** Everything built is backend. The MEMBER PORTAL (creator dashboard: value-used score, licenses, earnings, opportunities, security status) and the BRAND-FACING side (verified roster, ranked by value-used, with proof-packs) are the two missing product surfaces. Recommendation: portal MVP before marketplace polish.

**GAP 3 — Cold start never answered.** Creators need brands; brands need creators. The answer was already built in the layers but not stated as THE answer: (a) the GUARDIAN WEDGE — free impersonation/security sweeps for the first 100 notable creators: near-zero cost to ABBA, terrifyingly viral, and every sweep generates census data; (b) the CENSUS + RANKINGS make HAPi the reference before it has a single signed talent; (c) 10-flagship conveyor pilot from existing networks (religious and music verticals first — permission culture is native there).

**GAP 4 — Regulatory posture undefined (loophole).** (a) NDPA 2023: consent-first is already the architecture — formalize as the compliance posture and register with NDPC; (b) HAPI Bank must not touch regulated deposit/lending activity: run as the settlement/data layer OVER licensed PSP rails (Stripe-over-banks model), licensing only if/when scale demands it; (c) talent as contractors, not employees (misclassification risk); (d) the 18+ segment stays Tumi-style wellness positioning — payment processors prohibit adult-content rails, so structure stays clean; (e) contracts pack: management, licensing, booking, sponsorship, split-sheet templates — the legal moat that also kills the industry's handshake disease.

**GAP 5 — Data rights silent.** THE MEMBER DATA BILL OF RIGHTS: fan and creator data custody stays with the consent-giver; HAPi is custodian, not owner; export and portability on demand; nothing sold, only licensed with consent (know-before-use law applied to data). This becomes a marketing weapon — no agency in Nigeria can honestly sign it.

**GAP 6 — Concentration & key-man risk.** One platform ban can kill a line (lived: October 2026) — mitigation: owned-rail settlement + multi-platform by policy. ABBA/owner dependency — mitigation: every station documented (this doc series), decisions reversible, SealGrants delegable. Credit risk on financing — mitigation: full-cost accounting before underwriting, escrow-first deals.
