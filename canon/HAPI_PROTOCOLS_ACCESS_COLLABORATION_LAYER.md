Nigeria runs on protocol: order of proceedings, dignitary reception, MCs, red carpets, church/mosque etiquette. Two plays:
- **Protocol officers as a service:** trained, certified event staff (Academy product) — protocol, ushering, VIP handling.
- **Talent protocols (SOPs):** how HAPi talent conducts itself — stage, press, public, crisis. Protocol is trainable, so it is trained, so it is certified.

## 2. SPEECHES — THE GHOST ECONOMY BEHIND THE PODIUM

## 3. AUTOGRAPHS — SIGNATURES AS ASSETS

- **Memorabilia:** signed items with resale value — and the trust gap is authentication: Proof mints certificates of authenticity; the resale carries creator royalty (the resale-right inversion: original signer keeps a cut of every future sale — no platform does this).
- **Autograph sessions:** monetized meet-and-greets (photo + autograph packages at events).
- **Digital autographs:** signed digital copies (covers, ebooks, posters) — minted, limited, ledgered.
