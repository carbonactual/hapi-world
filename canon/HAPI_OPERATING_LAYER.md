## THE ONE-LINE TRUTH

The operating layer is not a new build. It is the EXISTING entity stack wearing talent clothes: Agent, Goal, Task, Swarm, SealGrant, Pulse, Proof, MemoryEvent — all already live in the ecosystem database. The conveyor below maps onto them directly.

## 1. THE TALENT CONVEYOR (lifecycle — every step is an entity state)

HUNT → WATCH → ACQUIRE → GROOM → TRAIN → MANAGE → PRODUCE → SHOW → MONETISE → ELEVATE → CONTINUE (RITES)

1. **HUNT (talent acquisition & hunting):** scouts + scout network (certified, tiered — the clipper ladder model applied to scouting). Where talent hides: campus drama units, church performance teams, open mics, raw TikToks with craft but no strategy, WhatsApp skit collectives, barber shops, dance groups. Scouting events (auditions as owned formats — the show IS the funnel). Inbound: the application pipeline. A scout's finding is a task; the sign-up is a record.
2. **WATCH:** evaluation is value-used from day zero — track what happens to the content AFTER consumption (saves, shares-with-intent, returns, purchases, behavior change), not likes. Scouting reports rank potential: ability, consistency, credibility, audience suitability, commercial ceiling, development needs. Never follower count (blueprint law).
3. **ACQUIRE (signing):** consent-first contracts (SealConsent). The inversion hardwired: creator keeps assets (likeness, catalog rights, accounts); HAPi holds liabilities (admin, chasing, distribution, risk). Terms recorded; rights ledgered (ContentLicense).
4. **GROOM:** a development plan per talent — BEING → BECOMING → ACTUAL mapped to Tree paths (22 paths, 33 steps). Mentor assignment (elevation path: seniors groom juniors — grooming is itself a rank requirement).
5. **TRAIN (Academy):** media training, on-camera, voice, editing, brand-building, business literacy, AI-assisted workflow. Activities: weekly drills, rep sheets, filmed practice with feedback. Completion = Proof minted (never purchasable).
6. **MANAGE:** management operations — calendars, deal flow, negotiation, admin, crisis line. Management is a SERVICE delivered by manager-agents, priced as retainers + share — not ownership.
7. **PRODUCE:** production operations — pipelines, shot calendars, edit queues, quality gates, full-cost accounting per asset (the true cost ledger).
8. **SHOW (programming & placement):** owned formats + placements. Content goes where value is harvested (payout-gap arbitrage); shows go where attention concentrates.
9. **MONETISE:** brand deal desk, monetization setup (per-platform payout eligibility engineering), commerce rails (WhatsApp settlement), fan economy builds.
10. **ELEVATE:** ranks on the trust ladder (value-used > pulse received). Badges, tiers, rates rise with Proof history. Ranks are never bought; they are underwritten.
11. **CONTINUE (RITES):** graduation to legend status, catalog estates, nothing dies unrecorded.

## 2. TEAMS & ORGANISATION (the org is six functions + guilds)

- SCOUTS (hunt), GROOMERS (develop), PRODUCERS (make), MANAGERS (run), DEAL-MAKERS (monetise), GUARDIANS (protect: security floor over every talent, always).
- Above them: the analyst function (value-used measurement — the engine room).
- Teams form as Swarms — task-bound units under SealGrants (scoped authority, budget caps, success conditions). This governance ALREADY EXISTS in the entity stack.
- Creators are members, not employees; guilds pool capacity (production guilds, niche guilds); guilds beat lone traders (Trade Canon law).

## 3. VALUE-USED — THE ENGINE (the watching system)

Every activity is a Pulse event (entity exists): content published → pulses received (attention) AND value used (downstream change). The ratio prices the talent, promotes the talent, renews the talent. It is the metric the entire conveyor runs on — and no agency in Nigeria measures it.

## 4. GOALS SYSTEM

Goals are Goal entities (exist): success_condition, progress_percent, next_best_action, value_created, due dates. Weekly cycles: set → execute via Tasks → review by Pulse → adjust. Every talent, team, guild and division runs on the same loop. Reviews are evidence-based (Proofs attached, not vibes).

## 5. SHOWS & PROGRAMMING

Owned formats: the flagship summit (annual), the rankings release, awards (Proof minting), discovery shows (audition formats — HUNT as entertainment), creator game shows, league formats (guild vs guild competition — the sports model applied to creation).

## 6. BRANDS SIDE

Brand acquisition desk (advertiser relationships as a managed pipeline), campaign ops, value-used attribution reports as the differentiator (brands buy outcomes, not impressions), retention on reports.

## 7. CONNECTIONS (the relationship graph — the compounding asset)

The constitution's relationship-graph bubbles: people, brands, platforms, gatekeepers — nodes with history, warmth, permissions. Introductions as a service (the Diplomat function). Every deal, collab, and show adds edges; the graph compounds while everything else depreciates. It is the one asset that can't be copied because it only exists as history.

## 8. ACTIVITIES (the operating rhythm)

Weekly: scout days, training days, content drops, review days. Monthly: rankings, payouts (HAPI Bank), goal reviews. Quarterly: market map refresh. Annual: the summit, the census, the awards.
