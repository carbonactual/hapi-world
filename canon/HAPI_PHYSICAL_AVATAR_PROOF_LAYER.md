## 1. THE PHYSICAL LAYER (offline creator economy)

The industry maps online attention; the durable money is increasingly physical. HAPi's offline grid:

- Events & appearances: hosting, MC-ing, guest appearances, meet-and-greets, paid cameos — pricing ladder by Proof rank, not follower count.
- Brand activations: street activations, campus tours, market activations (Nigeria's true social graph is physical: markets, campuses, churches, motor parks).
- Merch & physical products: merch-on-demand (no inventory), album drops, fashion lines; settlement via Paystack/Flutterwave rails.
- Studios, gear & venues: rental marketplace (capacity pooling per Trade Canon); studio hours as bankable CAPACITY.
- Physical proof-of-presence: ticketed events mint attendance badges (Proof), QR-gated communities, printed certificates with verification codes — physical artifacts that carry digital proofs.
- PERMISSION LAW EXTENDED TO THE PHYSICAL: venue photo/video consent releases, clapboard rights capture, event clipping licenses signed on the spot (QR → ContentLicense record). Every event becomes a licensed content mine, not a rights lawsuit waiting to happen.
- Divisions: 8 (events), 14 (infrastructure), 15 (rights), 11 (commerce).

## 2. AVATARS & HAPI WORLD (the virtual identity layer)

- AI avatars & virtual creators: global market (Lil Miquela etc.) — Nigeria has none of note. First-mover: a virtual influencer built on ABBA, likeness 100% HAPi-owned (no likeness-rights dispute by construction).
- Avatar-as-a-service: creators commission AI avatars of themselves — the ai_likeness license type already in the ContentLicense ledger. Consent-first: the creator owns the likeness; HAPi mints the license, never the other way round.
- HAPI World as the venue layer: the 1:1 Earth mirror hosts digital venues (Land & Space Registry cadastre) — virtual concerts, listening parties, meet-and-greets, masterclasses. Physical event + simultaneous mirror event = two ticket lines, one production.
- Digital twins of venues: event spaces register in the cadastre; their mirror doubles sub-lease to creators.
- Attendance in HAPI World mints presence Proofs usable as credentials, community passes, and status (hierarchy by presence, not purchase).
- Divisions: 10 (elevation), 15 (IP), 26 systems: Land Registry, Census, Archives.

## 4. BADGES, EVIDENCE & PROOF OF CLAIMS (the receipts economy)

- Claim verification as a service: "I have 1M followers / I sold 5,000 copies / I was booked by X" — ABBA verifies against APIs and receipts, mints a Proof. Brands pay for verification reports; creators pay for verified claims badges that raise their price.
- Badge taxonomy (honesty law — never purchasable): attendance badges, completion badges, assessed-competency badges, verified-claim badges, status badges (hierarchy markers — earned by value-used, never bought).
- Evidence chain: every badge carries its evidence_ids (already in the Proof schema) — tap a badge, see the receipts. Anti-fake-claims is a market: Nigeria's promo culture runs on unverifiable claims; the receipts economy monetizes truth.
- Badge portability: badges render across platforms (embeddable widgets) — the # identity travels; platform checkmarks stay platform-captive.
