## 1. Audubon Design System

AODS is the shared ecological design and interaction doctrine for Carbon Actual and HAPI World products. It uses the 435-plate universe as a design-addressable space and pairs product capabilities with habitat, motion, atmosphere, typography, and identity-mark systems.

Plate addressability does not imply historical verification. An unmapped plate remains unclassified until authoritative evidence is recorded.

AODS never overrides accessibility, identity, authority, evidence, privacy, security, regulated workflow, or product truth.

## 2. HAPI World Tier II

HAPI World may implement a parallel autonomous intelligence environment containing:

`# identity → registry → ABBA → agents → swarms/teams → missions → knowledge → value/Pulse → infrastructure → products`

ABBA is master intelligence and orchestration. ABBA is not the source of constitutional authority.

## 4. Coupling and derived entities

Agents may form capability couplings and shared BUNK habitats. A derived agent retains parent lineage and remains a candidate until the applicable certification path succeeds. Parentage never creates authority automatically.

## 5. Value and settlement

Work and capability are evaluated through evidence, Pulse and Value before settlement. HAPI Bank may prepare or execute a settlement only within the authority, regulatory and proof boundaries already defined by this Canon.

## 6. Security

Unknown is not synonymous with malicious. ASH retains anomaly material, ECHO preserves evidence and PHOENIX performs governed response/recovery. Permanent deletion is not the default constitutional response.

## 7. Repository law

Repositories consume versioned canonical manifests. Lower layers may implement and validate them but may not redefine the constitutional source. A conformance failure blocks the affected build surface.

## 8. Autonomy boundary

HAPI World can operate autonomously between required approval gates. Human authority and Seal requirements remain constitutional boundaries for consequential actions.
