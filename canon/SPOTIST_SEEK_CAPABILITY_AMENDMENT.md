# SPOTIST — Ecosystem Capability Amendment

**Status:** ACTIVE ECOSYSTEM AMENDMENT
**Canonical architecture source:** `carbonactual/carbonactual/architecture/SPOTIST_CANONICAL_CAPABILITY.md`
**Constitutional authority:** this amendment remains subordinate to `CANON.md`.

## Purpose

This amendment expands and clarifies the meaning of SPOTIST across the Carbon Actual ecosystem.

Earlier ecosystem material described Spotist primarily as a sourcing/resource-discovery layer. That remains a valid application, but it is **not the full semantic scope of SPOTIST**.

## Canonical meaning

> **SPOTIST is the ecosystem-wide capability for seeking: discovering, locating, identifying, matching, tracing, qualifying, verifying, connecting, assembling or monitoring whatever is legitimately being sought.**

The scope is open-world and may include people, relationships, identities, animals/pets, objects, property, vehicles, places, rare or unique items, books and collectibles, information, records, evidence leads, capabilities, human services, suppliers, production capacity, opportunities, offers, promotions, events, activities, adventures, resources, missing/lost items or connections, and other legitimately represented objects of intent.

## Product relationship

SPOTIST may exist as a reusable ecosystem capability, standalone product experience, module, API/service, agent capability, workflow or embedded seek interface. A product that consumes SPOTIST does not become the owner of SPOTIST's canonical meaning.

## Composition

- **Carbon Actual** provides the operating-spine contracts and constitutional interoperability boundary.
- **ABBA** interprets, reasons and orchestrates.
- **SPOTIST** performs the seek/discovery/matching function.
- **DESK** may expose SPOTIST as a participant-facing dashboard capability.
- **OMNI** may invoke SPOTIST for economic participation, trade, markets and investment workflows.
- **I/O** handles applicable value circulation, ledger and settlement.
- **HAPI** supplies the relevant human identity, authority and continuity surfaces.

## Sourcing remains a SPOTIST application

Supplier discovery, procurement, demand aggregation, manufacturing sourcing, capacity discovery, quotation, qualification and trade-related sourcing remain valid SPOTIST capabilities. They are applications of the broader SEEK primitive, not a restriction on it.

## Boundary

SPOTIST discovery does not itself grant authority. Sensitive personal discovery, identity disclosure, access to private records, regulated services, consequential communications, physical intervention, agreements and movement of value remain subject to applicable authority, consent, privacy, safeguarding, evidence, safety and governance mechanisms.

## Canonical invariant

> **The user may simply state what they are looking for. SPOTIST is responsible for adapting the seek to the thing being sought rather than requiring the user to choose the category first.**
