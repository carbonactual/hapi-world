# VOICE & VIDEO SURFACE LAW
**Carbon Actual Ecosystem — Canonical Layer (hapi-world/canon/)**
**Ratified: September 16, 2026 · Status: Law (Canon) · Sibling of TRADE_CANON.md and OMNI_BROWSER.md**

> **Position:** Voice and video are *obligatory product surfaces*, not optional luxuries. Every product in the ecosystem must be reachable by voice and must be able to speak and show itself through video. Products **tap** ABBA's shared voice/video engine; **no product builds its own second media engine.**

---

## ARTICLE I — THE VOICE SURFACE (input)

1. **Every product's primary interaction must be speakable.** A user must be able to describe their intent by voice, in their own language, instead of typing. TIP takes spoken sourcing requests. SPOTIST takes spoken seeks. Institute GPT hears student questions. ABBA hears commands. Any chat, form, or wizard in any product must have a voice-input equivalent.
2. **Speech-to-intent is the standard pipeline:** voice in → transcription (STT) → ABBA intent interpretation → product action. Products never parse raw audio themselves.
3. **Every product must accept voice notes** on channels that support them (WhatsApp voice notes, Telegram voice, web upload), and reply in text first, voice where the user used voice or asks for it.

## ARTICLE II — THE VOICE SURFACE (output)

4. **Every product must be able to speak.** Through ABBA's shared TTS engine, any product can deliver results, summaries, alerts, and briefings as natural voice output on supported channels.
5. **Voice identity is per-brand:** the ecosystem maintains one voice engine but each product may carry its own consistent voice persona (TIP institutional, Institute GPT patient, SPOTIST calm, ABBA commanding). One engine, many voices — never many engines.
6. **Voice replies are earned, not forced:** reply in voice when the user spoke by voice, asked for voice, or the channel is voice-native. Never send unrequested voice spam.

## ARTICLE III — THE VIDEO SURFACE (generation)

7. **Every product must be able to produce video.** Through ABBA's shared media engine, every product can generate: product demos, explainer videos, marketing clips, changelog/walkthrough videos, and social content — for any surface (site, socials, WhatsApp, ads).
8. **The 70/20/10 content-revenue formula applies ecosystem-wide** to generated video: 70% educational, 20% entertaining, 10% promotional.
9. **No product ships without a demo video** — the minimum viable video surface is one honest walkthrough of the product's primary interaction.

## ARTICLE IV — THE VIDEO SURFACE (live and interactive)

10. **Where a product's users meet in person or live, the product must support it:** video calls/video notes on supported channels, live walkthrough and support sessions, and screen-share guidance where the platform allows.
11. **Streaming is a monetization surface:** products with live audiences (Institute GPT classes, TIP market briefings) tap the ecosystem's live-streaming capability for reach and revenue.

## ARTICLE V — CONSENT, PRIVACY, SAFETY (non-negotiable)

12. **Recording consent is mandatory.** No voice or video of any person is captured, stored, or reused without explicit consent. Voice transcripts are data subject to the same consent/retention laws as text.
13. **Voice is biometric-adjacent:** voiceprints are never enrolled, stored, or shared without separate explicit consent, and are deleted on request.
14. **Deepfake law:** the ecosystem never generates synthetic video or voice *of a real person* without that person's recorded consent. Generated media must be labeled as generated when it depicts likeness.
15. **One engine, shared costs:** all STT/TTS/video generation flows through the shared media engine so every product gets the capability at the same marginal cost — capability for all, not for whoever can afford it.

## ARTICLE VI — TAP RULES

16. Products **inherit** this law and **tap** the shared engine (via ABBA orchestration). Product docs state their voice/video surfaces in one standard section: `## Voice & Video` — what is speakable, what speaks, what it can show, what it can stream.
17. A product that cannot expose a voice or video surface for a genuine technical reason must *declare the exception* in its conformance doc, with the blocker and the unblock condition. Silent absence is non-conformance.

*End of VOICE & VIDEO SURFACE LAW.*
