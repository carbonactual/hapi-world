## 1. YouTube — best payer in Nigeria, evergreen mine
- BEST SELLERS: evergreen search content (education, how-to, reviews — pays for years), Shorts (fast subscriber growth), long-form AdSense (YPP live in Nigeria), memberships/Super Thanks, music videos (Nigeria's biggest genre), documentary/culture series.
- GAP: education in local languages; niche professional tutorials (finance, tech, exam prep — ours already); channel monetization audits (creators leaving money in un-monetized back catalogs).
- OPENING: the back-catalog refinery — most Nigerian channels sit on hundreds of un-clipped evergreen videos. Fractionalize them into Shorts/Reels under license (Permission Law).

## 2. TikTok — biggest attention, no payout in NG
- BEST SELLERS: skits/comedy, music promotion/dance, food, transformation content; LIVE gifts (works in NG [VERIFY]); affiliate via TikTok Shop where live (1,000-follower affiliate bar; NG availability [VERIFY]).
- GAP: Creator Rewards skipped Nigeria [VERIFIED] — 6.3M NG creators with no direct payout.
- OPENING: the payout corridor — harvest TikTok attention, settle via YouTube/WhatsApp/Selar/licensed brand deals. This is textbook bottleneck arbitrage (Trade Canon).

## 3. Instagram — the brand-deal marketplace
- BEST SELLERS: Reels growth, carousel education, brand partnerships/ambassadorships (the real money), UGC creation for brands (no followers needed), broadcast channels.
- GAP: gift/subscription payouts not NG-available [VERIFY]; agency-grade deal negotiation for mid-tier creators.
- OPENING: the verified mid-tier directory — brands can't tell real from bought engagement; know-before-use grading sells trust.

## 4. Facebook — the sleeping giant (oldest, deepest NG reach)
- BEST SELLERS: Reels (fast organic reach right now), Groups (community commerce), Marketplace, page-admin promo economy (big NG pages sell slots ₦5k-100k+), in-stream ads where enabled [VERIFY].
- GAP: Groups for niches (exam-prep, trade, diaspora) are underbuilt; live commerce.
- OPENING: community-as-franchise — moderated, monetized Groups run on HAPi rails (our NOUN student communities are the prototype).

## 5. WhatsApp — the commerce rail nobody productizes
