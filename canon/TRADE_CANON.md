# TRADE CANON — The Canonical Law of Trade
**Carbon Actual Ecosystem — Canonical Layer**
**Ratified: September 16, 2026 · Status: Law (Canon) · Custodian: the Constitutional Layer (CANON.md)**

> **Position:** Trade is a *law of the ecosystem*, not a feature of any product. This canon defines what trade IS, its eternal patterns, and its complete type systems. Products (TIP first, others after) **tap** this canon — they inherit definitions, instantiate lanes, and comply with the laws. **No product owns, redefines, or overrides trade law.** Product-level documents describe *usage*; they never amend this file.

## PART I — CANONICAL DEFINITIONS

1. **Trade:** the verified exchange of value between counterparties across a corridor, settled under an agreed rail, evidenced by forms.
2. **Corridor:** any path — sea, land, air, rail, digital — along which value physically or logically moves between two markets.
3. **Lane:** a priced, schedulable instance of a corridor (e.g., China → Lagos). Products quote lanes; corridors are canonical.
4. **Chokepoint:** a corridor segment where transit can be denied, delayed, or taxed (canal, strait, border, gauge change, sanction wall).
5. **Emporium (Hub):** a physical or digital aggregation point where liquidity, trust, settlement, and dispute resolution concentrate. The caravanserai, the kontor, the bonded warehouse, the digital market node are one lineage.
6. **Pooling:** the canonical mechanism by which fragmented demand (many small intents) becomes a single large order. Pooling is the lawful answer to MOQ.
7. **Settlement:** the canonical extinction of a payment obligation. Settlement may be specie, specie-free (netting, drafts, tokens), or barter — but it must be *verified*.
8. **Credit:** the lawful deferral of settlement against evidence and trust. Credit is as old as the sea loan (*foenus nauticum*); it is lawful only when written and collateralized.
9. **Grading:** the canonical standardization of quality that permits sight-unseen trade. From the "Made Beaver" pelt unit to the EU Digital Product Passport, grading is the precondition of forward commerce.
10. **Deadhead:** any asset moved empty — the canonical waste. A one-way container is a wounded trade. Two-way flows are law.
11. **Counterparty:** a verified identity (per the canonical identity layer) capable of holding rights and obligations in a trade.
12. **Forms:** the canonical evidence of trade — documents and instruments (Part IV).

## PART II — THE SEVEN ETERNAL LAWS (verified across 3,000+ years of routes)

These patterns held on the Silk Road, the Trans-Saharan caravans, the Hanseatic Kontore, the Manila Galleon, and they hold on SWIFT gpi today. **They are law, not advice.**

**LAW 1 — Aggregation hubs beat lone traders.**
Liquidity and discovery concentrate. Individual merchants never walked end-to-end; they relayed through Samarkand, Timbuktu, Malacca, Bruges. → Every product must build *Virtual Emporia*: pooled liquidity, standardized RFQs, escrow, and freight aggregation per commodity class.

**LAW 2 — Never move physical money.**
Trade volume exploded wherever specie transport was replaced by netting: *feiqian* (flying money), *suftaja*, *sakk*, *hundi*, *piaohao*, *Wechselbrief*. → Settlement by ledger netting and credit drafts is canonical; specie-on-route is the failure mode.

**LAW 3 — Trust guilds beat cold matching.**
Sogdian guilds, Karimi networks, the Hansa, the Jinshang: self-governing verified networks with shared credit history and social sanction against fraudsters. → Verified merchant guilds with transparent trust scoring are the canonical trust layer; anonymous matching is not.

**LAW 4 — Sync capital to logistics seasons.**
Monsoon winds, hurricane seasons, frozen river roads: successful traders aligned capital release with the logistics window. → Seasonal shipping-window forecasting (Lunar New Year closures, peak-season rate spikes, harvest cycles) is a canonical product duty.

**LAW 5 — Bottlenecks create arbitrage; trade never stops, it reroutes.**
Constantinople's fall opened the Cape route. The Kiakhta silver ban restructured the tea trade. The Manila Galleon ran 100%+ margins on a tax-law price gap. → Regulatory friction is a signal: detect it, reroute legally, price it.

**LAW 6 — Standardized grading enables sight-unseen trade.**
"Made Beaver," rhubarb hole-boring inspections, sugar and tea grades. → Independent third-party grading (SGS/Bureau Veritas lineage) and digital grading certificates are canonical; no verified grade, no forward trade.

**LAW 7 — Never run a one-way leg.**
Triangular flows and Hanseatic round-trips beat Rome's one-way silver drain to India. → Bi-directional logistics matching (pairing exports with imports) is canonical; empty returns are taxable waste under the ecosystem's full-cost accounting.

## PART III — CANONICAL TYPE SYSTEMS

Any product dealing in trade must type its world with these canonical taxonomies. A product may add *instances*; it may not add *types* outside this registry.

### III-A. Route Era Types (the canonical time dimension)
1. **Ancient/Historical** — pre-1900 corridors (17 canonical: Overland Silk Road, Maritime Silk Road, Trans-Saharan, Amber Road, Incense Route, Roman–India monsoon route, Grand Trunk Road, Hanseatic network, Viking river routes, Carreira da Índia, Spanish Treasure Fleet, Manila Galleon, Triangular Atlantic, Tea Horse Road, Kiakhta Tea Road, Fur routes (North American & Siberian), Salt Roads, Rhubarb Routes).
2. **Current (2026)** — operating corridors and chokepoints: Suez/Red Sea, Panama, Malacca, Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Turkish Straits; Transpacific, Asia–Europe (Cape detour era), Transatlantic lanes; China–Europe rail; Middle Corridor (TITR); air cargo hubs; Northern Sea Route.
3. **Emerging** — IMEC, INSTC, Lobito, TAZARA revival, AfCFTA GTI corridors, Saudi Land Bridge, Kra Landbridge, Chabahar, Trans-Caspian, Arctic expansion, new fixed links (Fehmarnbelt, Kanal Istanbul).
4. **Future (2030–2040)** — Transpolar Arctic route, green/decarbonized corridors, autonomous & drone cargo, digital trade corridors (MLETR/e-invoice/e-BL/DPP), tokenized settlement, distributed (3D-print) manufacturing substitution.

### III-B. Transport Firm Structure Types
1. **Ocean carrier / liner alliance** (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, COSCO; Gemini/Ocean/Premier alliances) — vessel owners, publish schedules and rates.
2. **Tramp/bulk operator** — unscheduled vessels chartered per cargo (voyage, time, bareboat charter types).
3. **NVOCC / consolidator** — buys vessel capacity wholesale, resells retail, issues own house BoL. (The canonical SME entry point to sea freight.)
4. **Freight forwarder** — agent (acts for shipper) or principal (acts as carrier); multimodal architects.
5. **Digital freight forwarder** (Flexport, Freightos lineage) — API-quoted, instant-rate forwarders for small shippers.
6. **Express integrator** (DHL, FedEx, UPS) — owns the full chain door-to-door.
7. **3PL** — outsourced logistics operations (warehousing + transport). **4PL** — orchestrates multiple 3PLs, owns assets only in contracts. **5PL** — orchestrates supply-chain networks/aggregates demand itself.
8. **Customs broker / clearing agent** — lawful interface with customs authority.
9. **Drayage / cartage** — port-to-warehouse short haul.
10. **Rail operator / block-train runner** (China Railway express, TITR members).
11. **Air cargo: freighter airline, GSA (general sales agent), belly-hold capacity reseller.**
12. **Barge / inland waterway operator.**
13. **Ship broker & chartering house** — matches cargo to vessel (Suezmax, Capesize, Panamax…).
14. **Last-mile / courier / motorcycle logistics** (incl. emerging autonomous & drone middle-mile).
15. **Historical structural types (canonical lineage):** caravanserai operator, kafla/azalai caravan master, guild convoys (Hansa flotas), chartered monopoly company (Casa da Índia, EIC, HBC) — the ancestors of the above.

### III-C. Market Types (Local Markets)
1. **Emporium / entrepôt** — international aggregation hub (Malacca, Bruges; today: Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong re-export).
2. **Bazaar / souk** — dense urban permanent market (Kano, Istanbul Grand Bazaar).
3. **Periodic market** — scheduled rural/circuit markets (luachi, haat, fair circuits).
4. **Wet market / produce terminal** — perishables wholesale-to-retail.
5. **Wholesale terminal market** — bulk commodity node (Hunts Point, Rungis).
6. **Commodity exchange** — graded, standardized, forward-traded (LME, CME; canonical grading in action).
7. **Trade fair / exhibition market** (Canton Fair, Global Sources fairs) — periodic B2B liquidity events.
8. **Village / neighborhood market** — last retail ring.
9. **Night market / informal sector market** — unstructured but real liquidity.
10. **Digital bazaar / open protocol network** — ONDC-type unbundled commerce protocols.
11. **Managed marketplace** — platform-controlled (Amazon Business, Temu/Shein managed supply, Jumia, MercadoLibre).
12. **B2B sourcing portal** — Alibaba.com, 1688, Global Sources, Meesho-type reseller nets.
13. **Social commerce market** — TikTok Shop, live-stream trade.
14. **Bonded market / FTZ market** — duty-suspended trade zones (SCZONE lineage; the free-port tradition from Hamburg's Freihafen).

### III-D. Trade Form Types (Documents & Instruments)
1. **Offer/intent forms:** RFQ, intent (canonical *intent.created* event), proforma invoice.
2. **Transport forms:** Bill of Lading (ocean; e-BL lawful under MLETR), Air Waybill, CMR (road), rail consignment note, FIATA FCR, packing list.
3. **Trade finance forms:** Letter of Credit (+ UCP), documentary collection (DP/DA), Bank Payment Obligation, eURDT/URRF digital trade records, escrow milestone agreements, marine insurance certificate, Sinosure/ECA policy.
4. **Compliance forms:** Certificate of Origin (incl. EUR.1/AfCFTA preference), phyto/veterinary certificates, export declaration, customs entry, sanctions screening record, EU Digital Product Passport (mandatory July 2026 lineage).
5. **Settlement forms:** invoice, e-invoice (ISO 20022 lineage: pacs.008), hundi/suftaja-lineage credit drafts, netting statements, stablecoin settlement receipts.
6. **Legal entity forms:** sole trader, partnership, limited company, cooperative, consortium/syndicate, chartered/licensed entity (the chartered company is the historical ancestor of the licensed corporation).
7. **Canonical rule:** no verified form, no trade event. Every trade event in the ecosystem must reference its forms (per the Proof layer).

### III-E. Logistics Types
1. **Sea:** FCL, LCL (consolidated), reefer (cold chain), breakbulk, RoRo, tanker (liquid/gas/chemical), dry bulk, project/heavy-lift.
2. **Air:** freighter, belly-hold, charter, express courier.
3. **Rail:** block train, single-wagon, gauge-change transshipment (1520mm↔1435mm canonical chokepoint type).
4. **Road:** FTL, LTL, groupage, drayage, cross-border trucking (USMCA/TIR lineage).
5. **Inland waterway / barge / short-sea shipping.**
6. **Multimodal / intermodal** — containerized through-routes (the canonical modern caravan).
7. **Warehousing:** bonded warehouse, FTZ/SEZ, cross-dock, fulfillment center, cold store — the caravanserai lineage.
8. **Last-mile / urban distribution.**
9. **Incoterms families (canonical risk-split):** E (departure), F (main carriage unpaid), C (main carriage paid), D (delivered) — the modern descendant of caravan risk-sharing contracts.
10. **Emerging modes:** autonomous trucking, cargo drones/eVTOL, digital-file logistics (3D-print blueprint transfer + local production).

### III-F. Payment & Settlement Rail Types
1. **Correspondent banking rails:** SWIFT gpi (+ ISO 20022 pacs/camt messaging) — the Wechselbrief lineage institutionalized.
2. **Sovereign clearing systems:** CIPS (RMB), PBOC swap-line settlement.
3. **Regional/local-currency systems:** PAPSS (Africa), Buna (Arab region), Aani/mada (Gulf), UPI international (India), Nexus-connected instant payment systems.
4. **Wholesale CBDC / tokenized rails:** mBridge (MVP), tokenized deposits (Kinexys), regulated stablecoin settlement.
5. **Netting/draft systems:** the hundi/suftaja/feiqian lineage — ledger netting, platform-internal credit clearing.
6. **Escrow (milestone) settlement:** conditional release on verified events.
7. **Barter / countertrade:** canonical since antiquity; lawful when evidenced and priced.
8. **Informal value transfer (hawala):** acknowledged as historical infrastructure; in the ecosystem it must transition to licensed, audited rails (MSB partners) — consent-first, audit-first.

### III-G. Credit Types
1. **Documentary credit:** LC, BPO, eURDT records.
2. **Supply-chain finance / reverse factoring** (buyer-credit-led early payout).
3. **Trade credit insurance & ECA cover** (Allianz Trade, Coface, Sinosure) — the Lloyd's/Lloyd's Coffee House lineage.
4. **Maritime/trade loans** — *foenus nauticum* → modern trade loans and inventory finance.
5. **Escrow-milestone finance** (release-linked capital).
6. **B2B BNPL / net-terms fintech.**
7. **Cooperative & P2P credit circles** (the canonical mutual-guarantee model; esusu/tanda lineage).
8. **Development finance:** Afreximbank AFTRAF/AfCFTA facilities, ECA programs, SME export promotion schemes.
9. **Canonical constraint:** the global trade finance gap (~$2.5T, SME rejection ≈47%) is a *market failure the ecosystem exists to close* — by aggregating order data, escrow collateral, and pool credit to de-risk SME lending.

### III-H. Factory & Manufacturing Base Types
1. **Coastal high-tech delta clusters** (Pearl River, Yangtze).
2. **Inland cost-shift clusters** (Sichuan/Henan/Hubei — the "China interior").
3. **China+1 diversification bases** (Vietnam electronics/footwear, Indonesia resource-processing).
4. **PLI-subsidized national bases** (India electronics/pharma).
5. **Single-industry national bases** (Bangladesh RMG; Ethiopia leather/industrial parks).
6. **Nearshore corridor bases** (Mexico/USMCA; Morocco/Tunisia to EU; Turkey EU Customs Union; Poland/CEE).
7. **Zone-based bases** (Egypt SCZONE; SEZ/FTZ factories).
8. **Critical-mineral processing hubs** (China ~68%; emerging non-China capacity).
9. **Distributed manufacturing** (additive/local print hubs — future type).
10. **OEM / ODM / white-label / capacity-marketplace types** — other people's machines, fractional runs (the canonical manufacturing-tap model).

## PART IV — TAP RULES (How products consume this canon)

1. **Inherit:** definitions, the Seven Laws, and the type systems above are binding on every product that trades.
2. **Instantiate:** products create lanes, pools, quotes, guilds, and forms *of the canonical types* — never new types.
3. **Never own:** no product owns trade law. TIP does not own trade; TIP is a desk *tapping* this canon. Any conflict between a product document and this canon resolves in favor of the canon.
4. **Evidence:** every trade event must reference canonical forms and proof (per the Proof layer and CANON.md).
5. **Consent & value:** trades move value only under consent (SealGrant lineage); full-cost accounting applies — deadhead waste is charged, not ignored.
6. **Amendment:** only the constitutional layer (CANON.md custodianship) may amend this file. Products file usage documents, not amendments.

**Lineage note:** the hundi → Wechselbrief → ISO 20022 line; the caravanserai → kontor → bonded warehouse → fulfillment hub line; the Made Beaver → commodity grade → DPP line. Trade law is old; the ecosystem is its next custodian — not its author.
