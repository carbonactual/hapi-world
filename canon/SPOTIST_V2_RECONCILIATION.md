# SPOTIST — v2 Canonical Ecosystem Reconciliation

**Status:** ACTIVE CANONICAL RECONCILIATION
**Supersedes:** narrow interpretation of SPOTIST as only sourcing/resource discovery

## Canonical meaning

SPOTIST is the ecosystem-wide **SEEK capability** for discovering, locating, identifying, tracing, matching, qualifying, verifying, connecting, assembling and monitoring whatever is legitimately sought.

## Valid applications

Sourcing, procurement and resource discovery remain valid applications. Other applications include people/capabilities, family/relationship discovery, pets, objects, property, vehicles, rare items, information, records, opportunities, offers, events, adventures, experiences, missing/lost objects or connections, and future categories permitted by the open-world ontology.

## v2 requirements

The canonical Seek architecture now includes:

- first-class Seek objects;
- standing/continuous seeks;
- federated discovery-source fabric;
- evidence/provenance-aware results;
- explicit match/result states;
- seeker/holder/provider matching;
- compound seeks and missions;
- tailored personalization;
- no-result semantics;
- authority/privacy/safeguarding boundaries;
- commercial-integrity constraints;
- event/provenance compatibility;
- open-world extension without constitutional rewrite.

## Product boundary

SPOTIST may be exposed as a standalone product, module, API, agent capability, workflow or embedded capability. It may be consumed by DESK, OMNI, ABBA and future products.

The consuming product does not own the canonical semantics.

## Final invariant

> The user may state what they are looking for; SPOTIST adapts the seek to the thing being sought and returns evidence-aware, authority-bounded discovery rather than pretending that search is truth or authority.
