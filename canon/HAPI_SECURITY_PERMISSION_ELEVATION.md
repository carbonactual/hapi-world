## 1. THE PERMISSION LAW (know-before-use, applied to content)

Constitutional law: nothing enters or leaves the world unverified. Applied to publishing, this becomes the Permission Law:

**NOTHING IS POSTED, SHARED, CLIPPED, REMIXED, LICENSED OR SOLD THAT HAS NOT BEEN EXPLICITLY PERMISSIONED.**

- Every piece of content carries a permission state: WHO may use it, WHAT form (clip/repost/remix/exclusive), WHERE (platforms), WHEN (term), AT WHAT SHARE (revenue split), WITH WHAT PROOF (consent record).
- Permission is minted, not assumed. A handshake is not a license. A payment is not a license. A license is a sealed object: ContentLicense.
- Posting without permissioned rights = violation → Ash (waste exported as evidence) → Phoenix (malice protocol: takedown, evidence preservation, escalation).
- Exclusive content is a permission state with exclusivity scope and territory — not a folder on a hard drive.
- The permission ledger is the trust product: brands buy sight-unseen BECAUSE the permissions are verifiable (grading enables blind trade — Trade Canon law).
