# CLUSTER A — CREATIVE & ENTERTAINMENT

## A1. MUSIC & AUDIO
- Creators: musicians, DJs, producers, podcasters, radio hosts, audiobook narrators
- Content: music videos, freestyles, behind-the-scenes, live sets, podcast episodes, reaction/review shows
- Where it sells: YouTube, Boomplay/Audiomack, TikTok (harvest), Instagram, Spotify, radio syndication
- Money: streaming royalties, sync licensing (ads, film, games), shows/bookings, brand ambassadorships, merch, catalog licensing for clips
- Permission: HIGH — masters vs publishing rights, sample clearance, clip licensing, sync deals
- Security: impersonation, fake booking agents, leak theft, fake-stream fraud
- NG signal: strongest export vertical (Afrobeats global demand)
- HAPi play: ClipGuard retainers, licensed clipping of catalogs, sync brokering, show negotiation, fake-agent detection

## A2. COMEDY & SKITS
- Creators: skit makers, stand-ups, meme page admins, satirists
- Content: skits, reels, memes, parodies, crowd-work clips
- Where it sells: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, WhatsApp broadcast
- Money: brand ambassadorships (the NG skit-to-ambassador pipeline), live shows, sponsored skits, page-admin promo slots
- Permission: MEDIUM — repost culture is endemic; skit IP is routinely stolen by pages that monetize others' work
- Security: content theft is the norm; impersonator pages grow bigger than originals
- NG signal: among the strongest — the NG skit economy feeds brand deals
- HAPi play: content-theft sweeps + licensing (turn the thieves into licensees), ambassador negotiation, brand-deal vetting (fake briefs are common)

## A3. FILM & TV (NOLLYWOOD)
- Creators: actors, directors, cinematographers, editors, critics
- Content: trailers, BTS, scene breakdowns, reviews, auditions
- Where it sells: YouTube (full movies + TV), Prime/Showmax, TikTok harvest, cinema marketing
- Money: streaming deals, cinema box office, YouTube revenue, BTS sponsorships, audition/casting events
- Permission: HIGH — distribution rights by territory, clip licensing, image likeness in trailers
- Security: piracy (the big one), leak economics, fake casting agents charging fees
- NG signal: enormous industry, weak digital monetization of promo content
- HAPi play: clip licensing for promo, anti-piracy monitoring, casting events (Division 8), licensing of BTS

## A4. FASHION & MODELLING
- Creators: models, designers, stylists, tailors, thrift resellers
- Content: lookbooks, runway, GRWM, styling tips, thrift hauls, sewing tutorials
- Where it sells: Instagram (the NG fashion mall), TikTok, Pinterest, WhatsApp catalog commerce
- Money: brand modeling, collection sales, styling services, affiliate (retailers), school (sewing/styling courses)
- Permission: HIGH — likeness rights, image usage terms, campaign exclusivity clauses
- Security: fake casting calls (fees), image misuse/AI likeness theft, catfish modeling scams
- NG signal: very high; Instagram is the storefront
- HAPi play: likeness licensing ledger (ContentLicense: ai_likeness), casting vetting, campaign rights management, WhatsApp commerce rails

## A5. BEAUTY & LIFESTYLE
- Creators: makeup artists, skincare formulators, hair stylists, nail techs
- Content: tutorials, transformations, product reviews, before/afters
- Where it sells: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
- Money: brand deals (highest UGC demand niche), own product lines, academy (training is the real NG money), affiliate
- Permission: MEDIUM — usage rights in brand deals, review content ownership
- Security: fake-brand-deal briefs, product-counterfeit impersonation
- NG signal: high — UGC for beauty brands pays globally with zero follower needs
- HAPi play: UGC studio (product shot content for brands), deal vetting, training academies, licensing of before/after catalogs

## A6. DANCE & PERFORMANCE
- Creators: dancers, choreographers, cultural troupes, acrobats
- Content: challenge videos, choreo tutorials, cultural showcases
- Where it sells: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, event bookings
- Money: brand challenges (challenge creation fees), event bookings, cultural tourism sponsorships, teaching
- Permission: MEDIUM — challenge IP ownership (who owns a viral dance), music usage in videos
- Security: uncredited reuse, fake booking agents
- NG signal: strong (challenge economy is core to Afrobeats promotion)
- HAPi play: challenge IP + licensing, booking desk, challenge campaign management for labels

## A7. VISUAL ARTS & PHOTOGRAPHY
- Creators: artists, illustrators, photographers, digital/NFT-adjacent artists
- Content: process videos, gallery tours, presets, prints
- Where it sells: Instagram, X, Pinterest, galleries, Selar (prints/presets), physical exhibitions
- Money: originals/prints, commissions, presets/LUTs (digital products), licensing for brand use, teaching
- Permission: HIGH — image licensing is the whole business; AI training on artists' work without consent is the global flashpoint
- Security: art theft/reposting, AI style cloning without consent
- NG signal: moderate — monetization weak locally, licensing mostly informal
- HAPi play: the licensing ledger as the core product (image licenses with territory/term), consent-controlled AI licensing (ai_likeness/style), gallery events, digital product rails (Selar model)

## A8. WRITING & PUBLISHING
- Creators: authors, ghostwriters, newsletter writers, poets, screenwriters
- Content: serialized fiction (WhatsApp/Telegram status novels), newsletters, essays, thread essays
- Where it sells: Substack, Medium, X, WhatsApp communities, Amazon KDP
- Money: paid newsletters, KDP royalties, ghostwriting fees, anthology sales, audiobook adaptation
- Permission: HIGH — publishing rights, adaptation rights, translation rights; ghostwriting IP assignment
- Security: plagiarism scanning, piracy of paid newsletters
- NG signal: moderate and rising; WhatsApp fiction is a uniquely NG mass phenomenon
- HAPi play: ghostwriting agency (services economy), adaptation rights brokering, plagiarism guardian (Ash)

# CLUSTER B — KNOWLEDGE & EDUCATION

## B1. EDUCATION & HOW-TO (OUR PROVEN GROUND)
- Creators: teachers, tutors, course creators, exam-prep brands (NOUN BOT / CIBN BOT operate here)
- Content: tutorials, courses, live classes, exam prep, study-with-me
- Where it sells: YouTube (evergreen search pays), Selar, WhatsApp groups, own sites
- Money: course sales, cohort fees, subscriptions, certification fees, school partnerships
- Permission: MEDIUM-HIGH — course IP theft (screen recording + reselling is rampant), curriculum licensing to schools
- Security: content piracy, certificate forgery, impersonation of tutors
- NG signal: proven (our own live revenue lines)
- HAPi play: already operating — expand the model to new subjects (JAMB, ICAN, tech skills), certification with Proof mints, B2B to schools

## B2. TECH & CODING
- Creators: developers, no-code builders, tech career coaches, AI tool reviewers
- Content: build-in-public, tutorials, tool reviews, career advice
- Where it sells: YouTube, X, LinkedIn, GitHub, dev.to, Telegram communities
- Money: sponsorships (dev tools pay well), courses, consulting, affiliate (hosting/AI tools), job-placement referral fees
- Permission: LOW-MEDIUM — code licensing (open-source vs commercial), course IP
- Security: tutorial-based malware scams, fake job offers (very common), API-key leaks in content
- NG signal: strong — Nigerian devs are a global freelance force; tech content monetizes globally
- HAPi play: build-in-public channels for HAPi products, tech career academy, fake-job-offer guardian (huge NG scam surface), consulting bench

## B3. BUSINESS & FINANCE
- Creators: fintech commentators, personal finance coaches, crypto educators, forex traders, SME business coaches
- Content: explainers, market commentary, success stories, scam warnings
- Where it sells: YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, WhatsApp
- Money: sponsorships (fintech apps pay highest CPMs locally), paid communities, courses, affiliate (brokerages), newsletter
- Permission: MEDIUM — brand-deal disclosure rules; regulatory disclaimer obligations
- Security: HIGHEST RISK VERTICAL — investment scams hide as creator content; impersonation of finance creators drives real fraud. This is the guardian's home ground
- NG signal: strong demand, trust deficit
- HAPi play: the verified finance-creator directory (trust-metric graded), scam-detection as content (guardian brand builds trust), compliance review for fintech sponsors (paid to the creator by us on their behalf)

## B4. NEWS, COMMENTARY & GOSSIP
- Creators: journalists, commentators, gossip/admin pages, investigative channels
- Content: news recap, analysis, exclusives, community news
- Where it sells: X, YouTube, Instagram, Telegram channels, WhatsApp broadcast
- Money: sponsorships, subscriptions (Telegram paid channels), ad revenue, paid promos
- Permission: HIGH — copyright on reporting, defamation exposure, image rights
- Security: political harassment, state pressure, impersonation spreading fake news under your name
- NG signal: very high consumption; monetization thin (news is high-cost, low-direct-revenue)
- HAPi play: licensing of newsgathering to smaller pages, fact-check verification service, admin-page promo-slot marketplace (the page-admin economy, formalized)

## B5. LANGUAGE, CULTURE & HERITAGE
- Creators: language teachers (Hausa/Yoruba/Igbo for diaspora), historians, cultural storytellers, griots
- Content: language lessons, history explainers, cultural documentaries, proverbs
- Where it sells: YouTube (diaspora search), TikTok, Patreon-style member communities, schools
- Money: diaspora subscriptions (strong: diaspora pays in hard currency for cultural connection), licensing to schools, documentary grants
- Permission: MEDIUM — oral-history consent, archival footage rights, community IP (cultural property belongs to communities, not just recorders)
- Security: misrepresentation of culture, extraction disputes
- NG signal: underserved; the Atlas already exists in the ecosystem as a seed
- HAPi play: cultural licensing standard (community-consent framework — a canon-unique product), diaspora subscription products, heritage archive (Archives & Museum system)

# CLUSTER D — FAITH, COMMUNITY & CIVIC

## D1. RELIGION & FAITH
- Creators: churches, mosques, ministers, gospel artists, Islamic scholars
- Content: sermons, teachings, worship, gospel music, Quran tafsir
- Where it sells: YouTube (sermon channels are among NG's biggest video channels), Facebook (older congregations), WhatsApp, radio
- Money: donations/tithing rails (digital rails now normal), gospel music streams, event tickets (programs/conferences), media sales
- Permission: HIGH — sermon clipping is a huge gray market (clippers monetize sermons without consent); likeness of ministers; worship music rights
- Security: fake donation appeals (endemic), false-prophet impersonation, scam prayer lines
- NG signal: ENORMOUS — among the largest content categories in Nigeria
- HAPi play: THE flagship Permission Economy pilot candidate — sermon clip licensing for ministries (give clippers legal pipelines, ministries new reach + revenue, HAPi the ledger), donation-rail anti-fraud, verified-ministry directory
- Canon note: fits Consent Law perfectly; consent-first is culturally native here

- Where it sells: X, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp
- Money: grants, donations, corporate CSR partnerships
- Permission: MEDIUM — beneficiary image consent (dignity-first), campaign IP

- NG signal: steady; CSR budgets are real corporate money
