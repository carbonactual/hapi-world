## 1. ENTERTAINMENT & MEDIA FORMS NOT YET MAPPED

- Radio & broadcast: Nigeria's radio culture is massive and now streamable — AI-assisted radio segments, syndicated shows, audio-first creators. Radio remains the cheapest reach in the North.
- Nollywood & streaming economics: commissioning (Netflix/Prime/Showmax), day-and-date digital releases, film marketing as a service, cinema activations.
- Theatre & live performance: standup specials (paid streams), stage plays, live music — high-margin, physical, badge-minting events.
- Publishing: books, comics, graphic novels; Wattpad-to-screen pipeline (fan fiction as IP mining).
- Music deep cuts: sync licensing for ads/film, publishing royalties (MCSN/COSON registry angle — NG rights collection is broken; a clean registry is a market), beat-selling market (already huge on WhatsApp/Telegram — unbanked, unledgered), ghost production, sample clearance.
- Audio economy: audiobooks, voiceover market (AI voice cloning is eating it — consent-first AI voice licensing is the counter-play), jingles, podcast networks.
- Animation & kids' content: fintech cartoons, edutainment (HAPi Academy adjacent).
- Formats & talent shows: Big-Brother-class discovery formats as talent funnels; format licensing both directions (import/adapt and EXPORT Naija formats — the Afrobeats playbook applied to formats).

## 2. THE SERVICES STACK (B2B2C — the boring moats)

- Legal-as-a-service: contract review for creators (agency deals, licensing, NDAs) — flat-fee templates + ABBA review. Most Nigerian creators sign terrible contracts because lawyers cost more than the deal.
- Tax & accounting for creators: FIRS compliance, VAT on digital services, income smoothing — almost no accountant understands creator income in NG.
- Insurance products: equipment, event cancellation, income-protection, key-man cover for top creators.
- Health: burnout & mental-health support (creator churn's #1 silent cause), teletherapy partnerships.
- Personal security & privacy: doxxing protection, stalker response, secure ticketing (the guardian pillar at fame scale).
- Takedown & brand policing: NG has no functioning DMCA culture — takedown-as-a-service (ABBA detection + legal notices).
- Succession & estates: RITES for creators — catalog trusts, posthumous income (nobody in NG runs this).
- Ad-sales outsourcing: HAPi as the sponsorship sales house for creator properties (creators hate selling; sales is a function, not a talent).
- Travel & tour logistics: appearance management end-to-end.

## 4. ENTERPRISE & BUSINESS PLAYS

- Studio-as-a-service: banks, telcos, FMCGs are ALL building internal content studios — HAPi builds and RUNS them (faster than they can hire).
- Enterprise social ops: HAPi runs a brand's full social presence as an agency-of-record (the NOUN/CIBN engine generalized).
- THE FLAGSHIP EVENT: a Nigeria Creator Economy Summit — HAPi owns the industry's main room: census data, rankings release, deal floor, brand money, media coverage. The event IS the market power: the directory, the proofs, the brand relationships all concentrate in one annual room.
- Awards & rankings: an annual creator ranking (value-used metrics, not follower counts) + awards that mint Proofs — the industry's proof standard. Rankings make markets; whoever ranks, prices.
- Trade association & advocacy: collective bargaining with platforms & government (the COSON precedent: music rights went unpaid until creators organized; the same will happen with platform payouts).
- Government & institutional programs: Nigeria's creative-economy ambitions (ministries, agencies, funds) — contracts, grants, partnerships; HAPi as implementation partner (the census makes HAPi the natural data partner).
- Franchised physical studios: HAPi-branded studios on campuses and in major cities — the physical retail of the creator economy.
- B2B knowledge events: executive briefings on the creator economy for banks/telcos (they pay to understand the market their children live in).

## 5. LOCALIZATION AS A VERTICAL

Nigeria's content economy is English-first while the audience is multilingual: Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo content under-supplies massively. Translation/localization of winning content across languages = arbitrage on existing production. HAPi's clipping engine + AI dubbing (consent-first) multiplies every asset 4x.

## 6. THE PATTERN

Every layer added follows the same law: find what the industry treats as overhead (services, functions, boring things — legal, tax, rankings, events, localization) and run it as infrastructure. Overhead is where margins hide.
