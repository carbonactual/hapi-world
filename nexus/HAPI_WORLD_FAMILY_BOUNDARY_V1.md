# HAPI World Family Boundary v1

HAPI World Nexus is part of the HAPI World family.

## Responsibilities

- integration and routing infrastructure for HAPI World;
- capability discovery and federation;
- world/control-plane services;
- historical ecosystem audit and evidence where retained;
- adapters to ABBA, OMNII and external providers.

## Family contract

`B3C0M1NG/HAPI` is the human/API and identity boundary. HAPI World is the native AI world. Nexus provides integration/audit capabilities and must not define a competing constitutional layer.

## Shared invariants

OMNII governs universal contracts. ABBA provides intelligence/orchestration. SEAL governs authority. Proof precedes Actual. Pulse feeds learning. Future/simulated/unknown states do not silently become Actual.

## Experience and natural habitat inheritance

Nexus inherits the ecosystem natural-habitat and living-system experience doctrine. Integrations should preserve relevant relationships among place, habitat, people, infrastructure, movement, resources, events and ecosystem value rather than reducing them to disconnected technical records.

Where Nexus surfaces live operational state, motion and transitions should communicate real routing, state change, synchronization or recovery. Reduced-motion support, accessibility, performance, responsive behavior and truthful loading/failure/recovery are required.

## Free/open policy

New core Nexus capabilities must have a free/open-source, self-hostable or open-standard path. Commercial services are optional adapters.
