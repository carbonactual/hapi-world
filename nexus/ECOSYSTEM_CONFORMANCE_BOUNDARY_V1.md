# Ecosystem Conformance Boundary V1

HAPI World Nexus is a family/nexus integration surface for HAPI World. It does not define a second HAPI World constitution.

It must reference the canonical HAPI World Canon and consume OMNII identity, relationship, authority, capability, event and provenance contracts. Nexus behavior may connect worlds, entities, services and capabilities, but may not silently create alternate identity or authority records.

Human authority remains governed through HAPI/SEAL. AI-native execution remains within HAPI World boundaries. External providers are adapters.
