# Ecosystem Conformance Boundary V2

HAPI World Nexus is a family/nexus integration surface for HAPI World. It does not define a second HAPI World constitution.

It must reference the canonical HAPI World `CANON.md` and consume the current Carbon Actual identity, relationship, authority, capability, event and provenance contracts. Nexus behavior may connect worlds, entities, services and capabilities, but may not silently create alternate identity or authority records.

Human authority remains governed through HAPI/SEAL. AI-native execution remains within HAPI World boundaries. External providers are adapters.

The former OMNII integration references in this document are historical terminology; current semantic authority is Carbon Actual and the HAPI World constitutional layer.
