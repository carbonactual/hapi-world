# OMNII Integration Contract

HAPI World Nexus is an integration/audit surface and must consume, not redefine, the OMNII interoperability constitution.

Canonical AI continuity is:

`Human ↔ # ↔ Native AI / HAPI World ↔ authorized product environments`

Nexus may inspect, audit, correlate, and report integration relationships, identity resolution, capability trust, routing, and lifecycle evidence. It must preserve the distinction between resolution, verification, Proof, authorization, and execution.

`#` is identity, not authority. SEAL remains explicit human authority. Minting is distinct from tokenization: `MINT → TERMINAL`, then optional curation/tokenization toward `INDEX`.

HAPI World is the native AI existence environment and is not modeled as merely another peer product or transport middleware.
