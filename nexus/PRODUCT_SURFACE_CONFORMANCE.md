# HAPI World Nexus Product Surface Conformance

HAPI World Nexus, when presented as an actual participant-facing product surface, inherits the current Carbon Actual installable product-surface law and HAPI World constitutional boundary.

It must use one canonical responsive product body across browser, mobile and desktop delivery surfaces; support app-like installation/opening where technically applicable; preserve governed deep-link context; and retain identity, authority, state, evidence and provenance across updates and recovery.

Browser/device permissions, credentials, background work, caches and AI-agent tools are adapters and cannot silently expand human or organizational authority. Offline state must not create fresh consequential authority, value movement or settlement.

Exit, revocation, uninstall and residual state must remain explainable and attributable.

Historical references to OMNII product-surface law are superseded terminology; the current inherited product-surface contract is governed by Carbon Actual and HAPI World.
