# ANNEX: AfCFTA FRAMEWORK + FINANCE ARCHITECTURE + REVIEWS (Sept 4, 2026)

## AfCFTA reality check (verified 2026)
- 90% of tariff lines being liberalized on a staggered 5-year transition (sensitive products longer). Full implementation NOT yet complete — trading runs via the Guided Trade Initiative (GTI) for states that submitted tariff schedules.
- PAPSS (Pan-African Payment and Settlement System) is LIVE: instant cross-border payments in LOCAL currencies — no dollar conversion. This kills the biggest intra-African trade barrier.
- AfCFTA does NOT reduce duties on Chinese imports (extra-African goods). Its leverage for us: (a) sourcing from African manufacturers (Morocco/Tunisia textiles, Egypt, SA, Kenya, Ethiopia) at preferential tariffs, and (b) selling Lagos-consolidated goods across Africa (Accra, Nairobi, etc.) under AfCFTA rates with PAPSS settlement.

## Finance architecture — 3 compliant phases
Phase 1 (no license needed): Fractional group-buy. Buyers pre-pay for shares of a consolidated order (min share e.g. ₦5k-₦50k). It is their own money buying their own goods — co-ownership, NOT lending. Funds held by licensed rails (Flutterwave/Paystack escrow-style). The desk takes a disclosed facilitation fee.
Phase 2 (partner model): Embedded credit via LICENSED lenders (Payhippo: 3-hr SME loans, avg $1,300, 97% repayment; Moniepoint embedded credit). Desk originates + provides transaction data; lender carries balance sheet.
Phase 3 (institutional): Afreximbank ecosystem. Factoring / reverse factoring is Afreximbank's designated SME instrument for intra-African trade under AfCFTA. AfCFTA Adjustment Fund ($8-10B target, Credit Fund already closing deals). Path: desk transaction records qualify clients for factoring facilities.

## Fractionalize / decimalize / democratize — concretely
- Fractionalize: every consolidated order split into priced shares (a tailor takes 50 yards = 1/20th of a 1,000-yard factory order)
- Decimalize: landed-cost engine shows every cost line to 2 decimals per share — factory price, agent fee, freight/kg share, duty %, VAT, clearing, delivery, desk fee
- Democratize: WhatsApp-first, ₦5k entry point, public review scores
- Compliance guardrail: NO tradable tokens at launch — fractional co-ownership receipts only. Issuing transferable digital shares = securities regulation. Keep it simple contracts until scale/legal counsel.

## Reviews landscape (trust layer)
Reviews are fragmented across App Store, Google Play, Nairaland, Trustpilot, Facebook — NOBODY aggregates them. Findings so far: Womata (mostly positive app reviews, some refund complaints), Nairaland complaints about inflated agent rates and poor ops, Trustpilot warnings about agents demanding top-ups ($300-400 top-up scam pattern). Desk product: verified-review aggregation per agent/factory → public trust scores. Every fulfilled order adds a data point nobody else has.

## Revised architecture under AfCFTA
Two sourcing poles: China (price) + African manufacturers (AfCFTA preferential tariffs, shorter freight). One consolidation hub: Lagos. Pan-African distribution: GTI corridors + PAPSS local-currency settlement + licensed lender credit. The desk becomes the AI-native, fractional, transparent import/export router for African small businesses.

# ANNEX 2: INSURANCE + CUSTOMS COST ENGINE (Sept 4, 2026)

## Insurance — the law and the numbers
- NIIRA 2025 (Nigerian Insurance Industry Reform Act): ALL imports MUST carry marine cargo insurance from a NAICOM-licensed NIGERIAN insurer (local content rule). Non-negotiable, enforced.
- NEW: compulsory insurance for containers moving goods from Nigerian ports onward — penalties ≥ ₦1M for non-compliance.
- Market rates 2026: general cargo 0.1-0.6% of insured value; low-risk consumer goods 0.1-0.3%; minimum premiums (e.g. $250) make small shipments expensive on %.
- Desk play: ONE marine policy per consolidated order; fractional shares covered pro-rata; minimum premium amortized across all shareholders → per-share insurance cost near-zero. Desk files claims on behalf of shareholders (claims admin = real pain point solved).

## Customs cost structure — the real stack (verified 2026)
1. Import duty: 5-35% by HS code, on CIF
2. Surcharge: 7% of the DUTY (not CIF — commonly miscalculated)
3. ETLS levy: 0.5% of CIF
4. CISS: 1% of FOB (or FCS 4% FOB — enforcement inconsistent since 2025, confirm per shipment)
5. VAT: 7.5% of CIF + duty + all surcharges STACKED
6. Real example (5% duty, ₦10M FOB): total statutory ≈ ₦2M — ~20% on top even at the LOWEST duty band.

## Legitimate cost reductions (desk playbook)
1. HS classification optimization: same product can lawfully fit different codes/tariff bands; correct classification + advance rulings; never misdeclare (CCVO checks, fines 25-50%, seizure).
2. Preferential origin: ECOWAS ETLS = duty-free for ECOWAS-manufactured goods with certificate; AfCFTA preferential rates for African-origin — strengthens the African sourcing pole.
3. Shrink the CIF base lawfully: CIF = cost + insurance + freight; cheaper freight (sea, consolidation) lowers the duty base itself. Consolidation drops per-unit customs cost on every line.
4. VAT recovery: import VAT is creditable against output VAT for registered businesses — pass-through benefit to shareholders.
5. Kill non-statutory bleed: pre-filed NICIS II docs, accurate Form M/PAAR, no demurrage, all-in agent quotes (hidden terminal/handling fees), port timing (Tin Can vs Apapa congestion).
6. Sea vs air decision on TOTAL landed cost, not freight alone: air raises CIF base → raises duty.
7. Future: manufacturing concessions / pioneer status if the desk ever localizes production.

## Product implication
The desk quotes ONE all-in naira price per share: factory + freight + insurance (pro-rata) + full customs stack + delivery + fee. Two decimal places per line. The customs engine becomes a competitive weapon — importers currently guess; we compute.

# ANNEX 5: THE UNIVERSAL VALUE EXCHANGE MODEL (Sept 4, 2026)

## The vision (user-confirmed)
One desk serving the globe decimally to the person. Anyone — individual, neighbor, colleague, business, country — can post what they HAVE (offer) or what they NEED (request). Matching is universal: goods, minerals, human resource/skills, services, favors. Settlement is multi-modal.

## Every listing = OFFER or NEED, pinned to a location
Post via WhatsApp, web, or agent. Fields: what, how much, where, when, quality evidence, price or desired exchange.

## Seven settlement legs (all on one ledger)
1. CASH — naira, USD, cedis (PAPSS), RMB (WorldFirst/XTransfer), licensed stablecoins
2. BARTER — good-for-good / good-for-service, both sides valued in a common decimal reference unit
3. CREDIT — post-paid via licensed lenders only (partner model)
4. ADVANCE — prepayment / staged payments (30% deposit pattern)
5. SERVICE — skills and labor as the payment leg (human resource exchange)
6. FAVOR — small non-monetary exchanges, reputation-scored, building trust capital
7. CSR — corporate goods/services donated for community needs, documented for impact reporting

## The decimal reference unit (the core mechanism)
Everything gets valued in one internal decimalized reference unit (naira-anchored for stability) so ANY two things can be compared and matched: rice against plumbing work, scrap against school fees credit, minerals against machinery. The unit is an ACCOUNTING REFERENCE, not a currency — no issuance, no transferability outside transactions. This is the Value Conversion Engine (already canonical in carbonactual/hapi-world) applied to real-world trade, with the IOLedger as the transaction record.

## This is the thesis
The exchange = HAPI Bank's Value Conversion Engine made real. Existing competitors move goods for money. We move VALUE in any form between any parties, decimally, with a trust layer. Import desk, export desk, local circular loop, human resource matching, barter, credit, favor, CSR — one ledger, seven settlement legs, one matching engine.

## Guardrails (unchanged)
No token issuance (securities/e-money). Lending via licensed partners. Barter is legal. Mineral exports permitted. CSR documented. Every match leaves a review score.

# ANNEX 7: NAMING & IDENTITY STANDARDS + PULSE/ASH/PHOENIX SECURITY (Sept 5, 2026)

## The landscape (researched)
- ENS: 37.5M+ names, the onchain naming layer (PayPal, Base, Uniswap). ENSv2/Namechain L2 migration in progress; mainnet gas costs are real; Google indexes poorly; regulatory status unclear.
- Handshake (HNS): decentralized DNS root zone, own TLDs without ICANN, censorship-resistant.
- Unstoppable Domains: NFT domains (.crypto/.x), but a centralized company criticized for patent practices.
- W3C DIDs: v1.0 standard (2022); v1.1 Candidate Recommendation March 2026; DID Resolution spec due April 2026. Methods: did:key, did:ion, did:web.
- Verifiable Credentials: W3C standard; UN Transparency Protocol (UNTP) adopted did:web + VCs for SUPPLY CHAIN TRACEABILITY — directly relevant to our standards tiers, machinery inspection certs, and export provenance.
- GS1 verdict: emerging but converging; "proceed with caution" — ubiquitous adoption ~a decade out. Perfect fit for our blockchain-ready-not-dependent doctrine.

## Decision for the # namespace
The # remains native (Postgres + event ledger, free minting, zero gas), but is implemented DID-COMPATIBLE:
1. Ecosystem-issued #s use did:web format (resolvable via our domain, no blockchain, zero cost)
2. Key-bound portable #s use did:key where portability matters
3. Standards tiers, inspection certificates, provenance records issued as W3C Verifiable Credentials (aligned with UNTP for export goods)
4. Optional bridges LATER: ENS bridge for web3 presence, HNS for decentralized DNS root — bridges, never foundations
Never build ON ENS/HNS: gas costs, chain lock-in, poor discoverability, and democratized free minting dies on mainnet fees.

## PULSE / ASH / PHOENIX — the security mechanism (user-codified)
Every PULSE is MINTED as a container of whatever is sent back (feedback):
1. VALUE EXTRACTION: if the pulse content carries value → it is TOKENIZED (ledger value entries, non-transferable reputation receipts)
2. NO VALUE → the value used is stripped to the last value; the shell — ASH — is carried with the next outbound pulse and deposited there. Nothing wasted, nothing stored pointlessly.
3. MALICIOUS/HARMFUL → ASH activates into PHOENIX: the obliterator. Phoenix destroys or obliterates the threat — spam, scams, malicious payloads, fraud attempts.
4. ASH and PHOENIX are the ecosystem's security pair: Ash = dormant security shell (memory of what was stripped), Phoenix = active security response (the existing ABBA security stack — SkillScan, threat vetting, incident response — becomes Phoenix's organs).
5. WASTE EXPORTED AS SECURITY: stripped ash shells (threat signatures, scam patterns, blacklists) are carried to integrated EXTERNAL apps as protective intelligence — the ecosystem's garbage becomes its armor, shared outward. Zero waste, even in security.
Entity mapping: Pulse (minted container) → MemoryEvent/KnowledgeItem (tokenized value) → VaultRecord (ash archive) → Incident (phoenix activations) → SecurityAlert (exported ash).
